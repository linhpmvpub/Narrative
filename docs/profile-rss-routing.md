# RSS → source-backed factor → narrative

Local research feed only. `GET /api/v0/news/profile-rss` requires existing member authentication; the mobile gateway permits GET only. No canonical ingestion, graph approval, user subscription mutation or notifications. The existing RSS collector supplies current revisions; projection runs on feed reads.

Add `factor_bindings` to a versioned company profile: factor node, literal topic/context/change terms, optional exclusions, evidence, scope, review time, expiry (at most 90 days), and news age (at most 30 days). All three term groups must match title/summary with word boundaries. Returned spans reproduce original text. Bindings cannot anchor directly to a company ticker. Stage with the existing `engine.company_profiles` CLI; immutable versions and source/quote checks apply.

Paths use current relationship validity, not the profile's old snapshot. The existing structural company membership and directed bounded route rules apply. A route establishes conditional topic relevance only: every RSS card has RELATED_INFORMATION, UNKNOWN impact and graph_eligible=false, even when a mechanism exists. No direction, magnitude, order cancellation or price outcome is inferred from headlines.

Current revision payload hashes and enabled source status are checked. Available raw RSS captures must match; corrupt/mismatching captures withhold cards. Missing legacy captures are disclosed as NOT_ARCHIVED, never described as verified news. Source-reported publication dates are not independent publication proof. Same normalized URL is grouped; differing current titles/excerpts withhold the group until copies agree. Correction to an unrelated topic removes the card. Explicit withdrawal notices, source disabling, expired bindings/relations and withdrawn/corrupt profiles also withhold it. This is not a general semantic retraction detector.

Reads examine at most 500 current items, 30 profiles and return 100 cards; truncation is disclosed. Deduplication/conflict checks cover the scanned window, not the full archive. Term rules miss paraphrases and can match irrelevant contexts; source evidence supports the profile exposure, not every incoming news claim. Shared example narratives only; tenant-specific narrative matching and relevance calibration remain future work.

Local NVDA supply sample (ignored `source-cache/company-profiles/nvda-supply-2026`) uses an already captured SEC filing with original acquisition clocks. Eight of 42 components sourced, 34 UNKNOWN; four bindings cover memory, wafer fabrication, chip packaging and customer data-center buildout. Two research narratives cover supply resilience and customer infrastructure. No named supplier, exposure weight or production capacity is invented. One real article currently matches the buildout rule; two RSS copies collapse into one card, with missing legacy raw captures disclosed. Memory/wafer/packaging rules currently produce no cards.

Run `pytest tests/test_profile_news.py tests/test_company_profiles.py -q`, lint/types and normal CI. Optional local UI smoke: `.audit-venv/Scripts/python tests/browser_profile_rss.py` while the sample is fresh and local API/gateway are running. The smoke reads the local token without printing it. Raw sources, profiles, excerpts and private projections stay ignored; only code, synthetic tests and aggregate receipts go to GitHub.
