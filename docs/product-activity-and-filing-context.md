# Product shipments and separately cited filing context

`ISSUER_PRODUCT_SHIPMENTS` is a narrow informational binding for literal initial/strong shipment phrases, named product vocabulary and a trusted matching issuer CIK. Phrase matches must start a sentence or bullet. Sales growth and launches alone do not qualify. Quoted speech, attribution ambiguity, future wording, cancellation and negation hold the excerpt. Original offsets are preserved; no foundry exposure, capacity, financial impact or price direction is inferred.

Each shipment binding requires explicit narrative scope, a sourced product anchor, no aliases or counterparty reference, and literal supporting evidence. The shared vocabulary audit checks every phrase against same-issuer SEC evidence. The private operating-card projection supports this kind through existing canonical version, review, source permission and source-span gates; it does not automatically ingest or activate a profile.
